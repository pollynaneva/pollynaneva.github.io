Feminist Media Throughout History

I worked with in a team of 5 to create a digital collection of public domain texts throughout the first, second, and third waves of the feminist movement. We utilized XML and XSLT to mark up 11 different texts, focusing on mentions of feminist theory, power systems, religion, class, identity, and social theory. We then looked at the items marked in each category and the trends that appear when looking at them in chronological order. We also developed a website using HTML and CSS, we included reading page where each text was easily viewable and displayed for easy access to other researchers in the humanities.
